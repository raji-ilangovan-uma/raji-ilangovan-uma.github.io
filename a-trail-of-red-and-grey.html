A Trail of Red and Grey

I walk down memory lane
And don't recognize myself there
A version of myself untouched by grief
Painting the town yellow

They said it gets easier with time
But each day, my heart grows heavier
Anxiety fades into sorrow

Everything I touch turns grey

Sitting here with this heavy boulder
Not yet strong enough to carry it or move it
Tired of being grey, I look for another color

That's when I spot red

Full of rage
Sharp and bold
Lighter than the boulder
Easier to carry

I once painted the town yellow,
Now, I leave a trail of red and grey

Where's Amma?
